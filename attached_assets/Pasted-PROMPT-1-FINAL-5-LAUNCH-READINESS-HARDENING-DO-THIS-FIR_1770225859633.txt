CRITICAL REQUIREMENT (PRICE PARITY LOGIC)
We must maintain “pricing parity” between GBP and NGN offerings:
- Stripe (GBP) is for international/UK customers.
- Paystack (NGN) will be for Nigeria customers.
- The same internal service identifiers must be used for both.
- Do NOT attempt live FX conversion. Use fixed “price books”:
  - NGN price book (e.g., verification_ngn=10000, office_only_ngn=75000, office_plus_mail_ngn=150000)

These are intentionally “clean numbers” and do not require FX matching per day.
