What It Is
The CIE is an internal market research and analysis platform built specifically for Nigerian Exchange (NGX) equities. It applies a deterministic scoring model to every listed security and packages those insights for clients and internal analysts. It's Cellion's core technology product, licensed to Icon Stockbrokers.

The Scoring Engine
Every NGX security gets three scores, computed nightly:

Score	What it measures
IAS (Investment Attractiveness Score, 0–100)	Weighted average across Momentum (15%), Liquidity (10%), Quality, Valuation, Growth, and Financial Strength
RS (Risk Score, 0–100)	Derived from 90-day volatility, max drawdown, and liquidity risk
CS (Confidence Score, 0–100)	Data reliability — based on price history length, recency, and anomaly detection
Scores are penalised for missing fundamentals, low liquidity, or stale data, and are normalised cross-sectionally (ranked against sector peers) so they always reflect relative value.

Subscription Tiers
Access is strictly gated:

Tier	Price	What you get
Free	₦0	Market Pulse (ASI, oil, FX) + scores only for securities you already hold
CIE Subscriber	₦5,000/mo	Scores for all NGX securities, weekly buy/sell/hold recommendations, dividend intelligence, sector rotation signals
CIE Pro	₦10,000/mo	Everything above + enhanced momentum signals (daily/weekly/monthly), portfolio-level risk overlays, and priority analyst commentary
Revenue from subscriptions splits 70% to Cellion / 30% to Icon, enforced at the Paystack level on every transaction via a subaccount split.

Data & Automation
Nightly scheduler runs at 18:30 WAT to ingest end-of-day prices, then at 19:00 WAT to recompute all scores
Excel ingestion — admins can manually upload standard NGX75 reports to feed dividends, analyst notes, and market context
Model templates — scoring pillar weights can be adjusted via a Research Cockpit (Draft → Pending Approval → Active workflow)
Community signals — internal analysts can log Rumours, News items, and Trade Calls with credibility ratings (1–5) and sentiment tags
What Clients See (Intel Tab)
Market Pulse — macro snapshot: ASI index, Brent crude, Naira/USD
Security cards — each ticker shows a star rating (0–5), momentum dots (daily/weekly/monthly direction), and a recommendation badge (Strong Buy, Accumulate, Hold, Reduce, Sell)
Portfolio Intelligence — highlights signals specifically for stocks the client owns
Dividend alerts — "last day to buy" urgency badges for upcoming qualification dates
Upgrade prompts — free users see locked content with tier upgrade CTAs
What Admins & Executives See (Research Cockpit)
Top IAS gainers and high-risk alerts across the full NGX universe
A model editor with sliders to tune pillar weights
An immutable audit log of every score recomputation and model version change
Subscription revenue dashboards and payment history
In summary, the CIE is the platform's premium differentiator — an equity intelligence layer that turns raw NGX price data into structured, scored, and actionable research for retail investors, while giving Cellion a recurring subscription revenue stream on top of the brokerage commissions
