Who we are

The technology arm
of Cellion Platforms.

Cellion Labs is where regulated financial infrastructure meets product execution. We identify operators — brokers, banks, MFBs — who have the licence and market access, and we build the digital product they've been unable to build themselves.

Every application we build runs on Cellion One — Nigeria's compliance and transaction infrastructure layer. KYC, escrow, company formation, and bureau scoring are all available as API modules, not bolt-on integrations.

The partnership model

Four steps from first conversation
to live application.

01

Discovery

We map your market, your regulatory position, and what your clients actually need. One conversation.

02

Scope & terms

We propose the product, the revenue model, and the timeline. No ambiguity on who owns what.

03

Build

We build on Cellion One infrastructure. You review at every milestone. Typically 8–16 weeks to live.

04

Launch & grow

You take the product to market. We maintain the technology, add features, and share in the upside.

No retainer. No hourly billing. We only make money when the product makes money. If it doesn't work for you, it doesn't work for us.
